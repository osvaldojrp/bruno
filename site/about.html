01 The short version

From survival to
self-possession.

At twenty-one, Bruno Seratti arrives in London from Brazil with limited English, little money and a determination not to go backwards.

A massage job opens the door to the hidden economy of high-end escorting, and Bruno discovers that charm, discipline and nerve can carry him into rooms most people never see.

The work takes him from cramped flats to luxury hotels, celebrity encounters, Buckingham Palace, New York and the mega-yachts of Monaco. Money comes quickly; so do temptation, danger and relationships in which business and desire become impossible to separate. Along the way he adopts new names, new personas and a philosophy of survival all his own.

But Uncut Me is not simply a parade of outrageous stories. Beneath the swagger is a candid account of immigration, family loyalty, sexuality, ambition, love, loss and the cost of always performing. Each escape into a brighter world forces Bruno to ask a harder question: when every role can be reinvented, which version of you is real?

Told with shameless humour and startling tenderness, this is a memoir about making a life from whatever you have, surviving the consequences and discovering that the greatest luxury is finally feeling at home in your own skin.

03 The arc

One journey.
Five turning points.

The settings become more glamorous, but the real movement is inward.

  1. Brazil

    Family, hunger, resourcefulness—and the instinct to build something bigger.

  2. London

    Reinvention, enterprise and a hidden city opening one door at a time.

  3. New York

    Risk, ambition and the moment business becomes entangled with love.

  4. Monaco

    The height of glamour—and the realisation that luxury can still feel lonely.

  5. Home in himself

    After the noise, a harder-won definition of balance, identity and happiness.
